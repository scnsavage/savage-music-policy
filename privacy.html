🔒 SAVAGE MUSIC — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the SAVAGE MUSIC Discord application (“the Bot”) handles information when used in a Discord server. Please confirm these statements match the actual deployed bot before relying on this policy.

1. Information processed

To respond to commands and provide music features, the Bot may process Discord identifiers such as server, user, text-channel, and voice-channel IDs; command text and music URLs submitted to it; and track, requester, and queue information needed for playback. Discord and the hosting provider may separately process technical information under their own policies.

2. Storage and retention

The music queue may be held in memory while the Bot is running and may be lost when it restarts. This page does not promise that infrastructure or platform logs are never retained: Discord, hosting providers, and third-party services may keep technical logs under their own practices.

3. Use

Information processed by the Bot is used to handle commands, connect to voice channels, manage playback and queues, troubleshoot failures, and maintain service security.

4. Third parties

When you submit a music URL or request playback, relevant information may be sent to services used to retrieve or stream that content. Discord processes interactions and account/server information under Discord’s own policies. We do not control third-party data practices.

5. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect Bot credentials and access, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Never share a Bot token or other secret in public channels. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information to the Bot.

6. Your choices and questions

You can stop using the Bot or ask a server administrator to remove it. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the official community or support channel where the Bot was provided.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated by publishing a revised version on this page.
